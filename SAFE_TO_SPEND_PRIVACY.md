# Safe to Spend Privacy Policy

**Effective November 13, 2026**

Safe to Spend: Payday Planner is a free personal-finance organizer. It has no account, no ads and no tracking, and **it has no internet access at all**: the app doesn't have Android's internet permission, so it can't send your information anywhere.

## What the app stores, and where
Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your phone: your cash amount, paycheck, income, bills and bill payments, debts, subscriptions, activity entries and settings. The developer has no server and never receives any of it.

The app excludes your records from Android's cloud backup and phone-to-phone transfer. Your data only leaves the phone if you choose to:
- **Back up** to a file you save (optionally locked with a password using AES-256-GCM encryption),
- **Export** a spreadsheet (CSV), or
- **Share an error report** after a crash. The report contains only the type of error and where in the code it happened (numbers are removed, and no amounts, names or dates are included). It's sent only through an app you pick, such as email.

Those files go wherever you save or send them. Treat them as private, because backups and exports contain your money details.

## Permissions
- **Notifications**: only if you turn on bill reminders. Reminders are scheduled on the phone. The lock screen shows only "Bills need attention."
- **Run at startup and keep the device awake briefly**: so reminders still work after a restart.

## What the app doesn't do
No bank logins or credentials, no account, no analytics, no advertising, no selling or sharing of data, no location, contacts, camera or microphone access.

## Not financial advice
Safe to Spend does arithmetic on what you enter. Its numbers are estimates, not your bank balance and not financial advice.

## Children
The app is meant for adults and isn't directed to children under 13.

## Deleting your data
Use **Settings → Delete all my data**, or uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings. Because the developer never receives your data, there's nothing stored elsewhere to delete.
