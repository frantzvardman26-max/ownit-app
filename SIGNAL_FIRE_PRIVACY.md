# Signal Fire: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 14, 2026*

Signal Fire is a free app made by Frantz Vardman for staying in touch with nearby people when there's no internet or cell service.

## How it works
Signal Fire connects directly to nearby phones that also run Signal Fire, using Bluetooth and Wi-Fi (Google Nearby Connections). **It has no internet access**: it is built without Android's internet permission, and nothing is sent to any server, including ours.

## What nearby people can see
Anything you post (messages, supply posts, place pins, medic beacons, and your nickname) is shared with nearby phones running Signal Fire, and they can pass it on to other phones. **Treat everything you post as public to people around you.** Never post your home address or private information.

## Location
Your location is used on your phone to show distance and direction to shared places. It is only shared when you choose to drop a pin or send a medic beacon with location.

## What's stored
Posts and your nickname are kept on your phone only. Posts older than 48 hours are deleted automatically. Signal Fire is excluded from cloud backups.

## What Signal Fire does NOT do
No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no data sales.

## Deleting your data
Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's settings.

## Safety
Signal Fire doesn't replace emergency services. Messages from other people are unverified.
