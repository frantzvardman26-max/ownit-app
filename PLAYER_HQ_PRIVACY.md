# Vindie Player HQ: privacy

Vindie Player HQ keeps your goals, sessions, garage, money and notes **on your phone**. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you tap ☁️ Save, which copies them to your own shop account so you can get them back on another phone.

- No ads, no trackers, no analytics, no selling of data.
- No account needed to use it.
- The "Session in progress" notification is made on your phone and never leaves it.
- Backups go to your phone's Downloads folder; you choose where they go from there.
- Nothing from any game is used (no art, maps or data). Player HQ is independent and not affiliated with any game publisher.
