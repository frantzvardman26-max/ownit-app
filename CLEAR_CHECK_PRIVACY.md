# Clear Check: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Clear Check is a free business self-audit app made by Frantz Vardman.

**What it collects: nothing.** Your answers, scores and past check-ups are stored only on your phone. The app has no internet permission, no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Backups are turned off, so your answers aren't copied to cloud backups.

**When something leaves your phone:** only when you choose to save or share the PDF report. You pick where it goes.

**Links:** each question links to an official source (for example IRS.gov or FTC.gov). Tapping one opens your web browser, which follows that website's own privacy policy.

**Calendar reminder:** if you tap "Remind me", your own calendar app opens with a suggested monthly event. Clear Check can't read your calendar.

**Deleting your data:** tap "Start over" in the report, or uninstall the app.

**Not advice:** Clear Check gives general information, not legal, tax or financial advice.
