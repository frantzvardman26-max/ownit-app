# Pure Beats: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 14, 2026*

Pure Beats is a free beat maker made by Frantz Vardman.

## What the app stores
- The beat you're working on and its stems, in the app's temporary storage.
- Beats you choose to save go to your phone's **Music/Pure Beats** folder, where you control them.
- The producer name you type for certificates.

## What the app does NOT do
- **It has no internet access.** Every sound is generated on your phone, and the app is built without Android's internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
- No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no data sales.
- It does not record from your microphone.

## Your beats
Beats you make are yours. Pure Beats claims no rights in them.

## Deleting your data
Uninstall the app to remove its temporary files. Saved beats stay in Music/Pure Beats until you delete them.

## Children
Pure Beats collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
