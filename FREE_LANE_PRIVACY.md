# Free Lane: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 21, 2026_

Free Lane is a free Android app store made by Frantz Vardman (J & F Studios).

## The short version

- No ads, no analytics companies, and nothing is ever sold or shared.
- Free Lane needs a free members account (your email). The same login works in all of Frantz Vardman's apps on your phone.
- Frantz keeps an activity log of your account: when you sign in and out, which apps you install or update through Free Lane, and when. It's kept in his own database, only he sees it, and it's deleted after 180 days.

## Your account and the activity log

When you sign in, sign out, or install or update an app through Free Lane, the shop (shop.frantzmichaelsthilairevardman.com) records the time, what happened, which app, your account email or ID, a rough location (country, region and city, worked out from your connection, not GPS) and your device type. It also notes when you were last active, so Frantz can see who is online. This is used only to run and protect the store and support you. Sign out any time under You. To delete your account and its log, use "Your account (sign out or delete it)" in the app.

## What Free Lane connects to

- **The store catalog**, from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com), where the apps are published. Like any website, GitHub can see the request.
- **App files**, from the shop with your sign-in, so each install or update is recorded in the activity log above.

**Beta testers only.** If you add a beta tester code (Updates → Beta tester), each time Free Lane installs or updates an app it sends the shop your tester code, which app, its version, and whether it was an install or an update, so Frantz can see what you're testing. Remove the code any time to stop. Nothing is sent without a code.
- **App images** (icons and screenshots), from the same place.
- The **Submit** button opens the submission page on shop.frantzmichaelsthilairevardman.com in your browser.

## What stays on your phone

- A copy of the catalog and images, so the store opens quickly and works offline.
- Your settings (for example "Update automatically").
- Downloaded app files, only until they're installed; then they're deleted.

## Installing and updating apps

Free Lane asks Android for permission to install apps. Before installing, it checks each file's SHA-256 fingerprint against the catalog and refuses any file that doesn't match. To show Install, Update or Open, it checks which store apps are on your phone; that check happens on the phone and is never sent anywhere.

## Notifications

If you allow them, Free Lane posts a notification when updates are ready. You can turn this off in Android settings.

## Apps from other developers

Each app in Free Lane has its own privacy policy. Free Lane reviews apps before listing them, but each developer is responsible for their app.
