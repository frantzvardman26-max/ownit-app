# Free Lane: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 15, 2026_

Free Lane is a free Android app store made by Frantz Vardman (J & F Studios).

## The short version

- No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
- Free Lane does not collect or send any information about you.

## What Free Lane connects to

- **The store catalog and app files**, downloaded from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com and github.com), where the apps are published. Like any website, GitHub can see the request (your IP address and that a file was downloaded). Free Lane sends nothing else.
- **App images** (icons and screenshots), from the same place.
- The **Submit** button opens the submission page on shop.frantzmichaelsthilairevardman.com in your browser.

## What stays on your phone

- A copy of the catalog and images, so the store opens quickly and works offline.
- Your settings (for example "Update automatically").
- Downloaded app files, only until they're installed; then they're deleted.

## Installing and updating apps

Free Lane asks Android for permission to install apps. Before installing, it checks each file's SHA-256 fingerprint against the catalog and refuses any file that doesn't match. To show Install, Update or Open, it checks which store apps are on your phone; that check happens on the phone and is never sent anywhere.

## Notifications

If you allow them, Free Lane posts a notification when updates are ready. You can turn this off in Android settings.

## Apps from other developers

Each app in Free Lane has its own privacy policy. Free Lane reviews apps before listing them, but each developer is responsible for their app.
