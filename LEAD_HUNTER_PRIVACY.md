# Lead Hunter: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 15, 2026_

Lead Hunter is made by Frantz Vardman (J & F Studios). It helps roofing and window sales reps find and follow up on homeowner leads.

## The short version

- No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
- Your leads, notes and voice notes are stored on your phone. We never receive them.

## What Lead Hunter connects to, and why

- **Public property records.** To find properties, Lead Hunter looks up public parcel data from government map servers (for example, the Texas statewide parcel service and county appraisal districts). It sends the area or address you search for. These are public records run by those agencies.
- **Storm reports.** It reads public severe-weather reports from NOAA's Storm Prediction Center to show recent hail and wind areas.
- **Phone lookup (optional, off unless you add a key).** If you enter your own Tracerfy API key, Lead Hunter sends a lead's name and address to Tracerfy to find a phone number. Tracerfy is a separate company with its own privacy policy and charges your account per result. Without a key, nothing is sent.
- **Updates.** It checks GitHub for a newer version of the app.
- **Links you tap** (maps, search, the Do Not Call registry, county sites) open in your browser or maps app.

## What stays on your phone

- Leads, statuses, notes, reminders and any CSV files you import.
- **Voice notes.** The microphone is used only when you tap record on a lead. Recordings are saved on your phone and never uploaded.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage erases this data.

## Your responsibilities

Lead lists can include personal information about homeowners. Use them lawfully: respect the National Do Not Call Registry and your state's telemarketing and solicitation rules.

## Children

Lead Hunter is a business tool and isn't meant for children under 13.
