# Vindie News: privacy policy

Vindie News shows the latest news from the Frantz Vardman Shop (shop.frantzmichaelsthilairevardman.com/news).

- **What it collects:** nothing. No account, no tracking, no ads, no analytics. The app only downloads the news issues.
- **Email sign-up (optional):** if you type your email in the Subscribe box, it is stored privately by the shop to send you Vindie News emails. It is never sold or shared. Unsubscribe any time at shop.frantzmichaelsthilairevardman.com/unsubscribe.
- **On your phone:** the app remembers which issues you've already seen (to show NEW badges). That stays on your phone.
- **Links:** product pages, checkout and other websites open in your browser and follow their own policies.

Contact: through the shop's contact page. © 2026 Frantz Vardman.
