# Reclaim: Name & Privacy Defense — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 15, 2026_

Reclaim is a free Android app made by Frantz Vardman that helps you log evidence of harassment or misuse of your name and information, write removal and stop-contact letters, and find help.

## What Reclaim collects

Nothing. Reclaim has no account, no ads, no analytics, and **no internet permission**, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

## What stays on your phone

The evidence log, the details you enter (name, email, address) and the letters and notes you save are stored **only on your phone**, encrypted with AES-256 using a key kept in Android's hardware-backed Keystore.

## When information leaves your phone

Only when you choose to print, save or share a letter. Where it goes is up to you (your printer, a file you pick, or an app you share it to). Links in the Help tab open in your own web browser; Reclaim itself never connects to the internet.

## Deleting your information

Use **Learn → Erase everything**, or uninstall the app. Both delete everything Reclaim stored. Phone backups don't include Reclaim's data.

## Children

Reclaim is not directed at children under 13.

## Not legal advice

Reclaim provides general information about consumer rights under U.S. and California law. It is not a law firm and does not give legal advice.
