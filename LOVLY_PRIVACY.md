# Lovly: Credit Repair — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 15, 2026_

Lovly is a free Android app made by Frantz Vardman that helps you learn your credit rights, write dispute letters, and track deadlines.

## What Lovly collects

Nothing. Lovly has no account, no ads, no analytics, and **no internet permission**, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

## What stays on your phone

The name, address, optional phone number, date of birth and last four digits of your Social Security number you enter, and the letters, dates and notes you save, are stored **only on your phone**, encrypted with AES-256 using a key kept in Android's hardware-backed Keystore. Lovly never asks for or stores your full Social Security number.

## When information leaves your phone

Only when you choose to print, save or share a letter. Where it goes is up to you (your printer, a file you pick, or an app you share it to). Adding a deadline to your calendar opens your own calendar app with the details filled in; you choose whether to save it.

## Deleting your information

Use **Learn → Erase everything**, or uninstall the app. Both delete everything Lovly stored. Phone backups don't include Lovly's data.

## Children

Lovly is not directed at children under 13.

## Not legal advice

Lovly provides general information about consumer rights under U.S. law. It is not a law firm and does not give legal advice.
