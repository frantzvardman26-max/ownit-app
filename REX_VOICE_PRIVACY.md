# Rex Voice: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 15, 2026_

Rex Voice is made by Frantz Vardman (J & F Studios). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## The short version

- Rex Voice has **no internet permission**. It cannot send your recordings, your scripts or anything else off your phone.
- There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- Your recordings stay in the app's private storage until you share them, save them or delete them.

## What the app keeps on your phone

- **Voice recordings** you make on the Record tab, with the name you give each voice and the speaker's name.
- **Consent recordings**: the spoken statement in which the speaker agrees to their voice being used. A voice can't be sent to a cloning engine without one.
- **Narrations** you create from a script, until you replace them.

All of this lives in storage only Rex Voice can read. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage erases it. You can delete any voice, along with its consent recording, from the Voice Vault.

## Microphone

Rex Voice asks for the microphone only to record when you tap Record. It never records in the background.

## Text-to-speech

The Narrator uses your phone's own text-to-speech voices. Rex Voice only uses voices that work offline. Your phone's text-to-speech service is provided by your phone's maker or Google and follows its own policy.

## Sharing and saving

When you tap Share or Save, the file goes where you choose (another app, or your Music folder). After that, the app you picked controls it.

## Voice cloning

This version does not clone voices. If a cloning service is added later, it will be off until you turn it on, this policy will be updated first, and the app will tell you which service it is and what is sent to it before anything leaves your phone.

## Children

Rex Voice isn't designed for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their information.
