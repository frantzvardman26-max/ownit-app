# Frantz Vardman

![Frantz Vardman, app developer and music producer](frantz-vardman.jpg)

**Frantz Vardman** is an independent Android app developer, music producer and digital product maker. He is the founder of [Frantz Vardman Shop](https://shop.frantzmichaelsthilairevardman.com/about) and [Free Lane](https://fv-freelane.pages.dev/), a free Android app store, and has built 12 free, offline Android apps using AI and free cloud tools. As **AceThe_Kid**, he produces original beats with full stems.
