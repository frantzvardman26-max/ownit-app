# Palm Distribute (Beta): privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Palm Distribute Music is a merch service for independent artists by Frantz Vardman. The app is in **beta**.

- **What the app is:** a window onto the Palm Distribute website (fv-palm.pages.dev). It needs internet.
- **What you give us:** only what you type when you apply or set up your store (artist name, email, links, bio, logo and the products you want). It's used only to set up and run your merch store.
- **Payments** are handled by Stripe in your browser. The app never sees your card.
- **No tracking, no selling.** The app adds no analytics or ad trackers, and your information is never sold.

Questions or to delete your information: https://shop.frantzmichaelsthilairevardman.com/support/
