# Own It Privacy Policy

**Effective November 13, 2026**

Own It is a free app for artists and managers. There's no account, no sign-up, no ads, and no tracking. This page explains exactly what stays on your phone and the few times Own It goes online.

## What stays on your phone

Everything you put in Own It stays on your phone: your scanned contracts, documents, drafts, deadlines, business records (invoices, money orders, shows, songs, roster, store products and sales, collaborator splits), your fan list, your press kit, your "My info," and your settings. None of it is uploaded to us. We don't have a server that stores your data.

**Backups** are files you create yourself. They go wherever you choose to save them, like Google Drive, email or your phone's storage. If you turn on **Automatic backup**, Own It saves a backup file about once a week to the folder you picked, and nowhere else. If Android's own backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may also include Own It's data in your Google account backup.

**Your fan list** stays on your phone. When you send a drop, Own It opens your own texting or email app with the message; Own It never sends or sees those messages.

**Your store** is a list on your phone. Buyers pay through the checkout link you add (for example Stripe, Shopify, Bandcamp, Cash App or PayPal). Own It never handles payments or sees buyer details.

## When Own It goes online

Own It only connects to the internet in these cases:

1. **Checking for updates.** Own It asks GitHub (where the app is published) whether a newer version exists, and downloads it if you tap Update. GitHub sees your IP address the way any website does. See GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
2. **Feedback, only when you tap Send.** What you type (the type of feedback, star rating, message, what you'd like to see, and any contact you choose to add), plus your app version, phone model, Android version, app language and whether you use the artist or manager profile, is delivered to the developer as a private notification through ntfy (https://ntfy.sh). Don't include private information unless you want a reply. ntfy's privacy policy: https://ntfy.sh/docs/privacy/
3. **Problem reports, only if you choose to send one.** After a crash, Own It offers to share a report containing the error and your phone model. You pick how to send it (for example, by text or email) with your phone's share menu. Nothing is sent unless you send it.
4. **Own It Artists directory, only if you choose to be listed.** When you tap Send for approval, the listing you typed (name, what you sell, city, store link and one line about you) is sent to the developer through ntfy. If approved, it is published on a public list anyone can see, on GitHub. Viewing the directory downloads that public list from GitHub. To change or remove your listing, send an update or use Send feedback.
5. **Links you tap.** Official sources and other links open in your web browser.

## Reading photos and PDFs

When you scan a photo or screenshot, Own It reads the text on your phone using Google's ML Kit. Google's ML Kit documentation says its Android libraries may send Google limited technical data, such as device and app information, performance metrics and error codes: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure

## App lock

If you turn on App lock, Own It uses your phone's own fingerprint, face or screen lock. Own It never sees or stores your fingerprint, face or PIN.

## Children

Own It isn't directed to children under 13.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling Own It, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes everything it kept on your phone. To ask about feedback you sent, use Send feedback in the app.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date, and important changes will be mentioned in the app's "What's new."

## Contact

Use **Menu → Send feedback** in the app.
