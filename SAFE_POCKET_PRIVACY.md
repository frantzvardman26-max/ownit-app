# Safe Pocket: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 14, 2026*

Safe Pocket is made by Frantz Vardman. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **nothing leaves your phone.**

## What Safe Pocket stores
- Photos and files of documents you choose to add (for example an ID or birth certificate).
- Appointments, contacts and checklist progress you type in.
- Your language choice and, if you set one, a scrambled (hashed) version of your PIN. The PIN itself is never stored.

## Where it's stored
Only on your phone. Documents and your records are encrypted with AES-256 using a key created inside your phone's secure hardware (Android Keystore). The key never leaves the phone.

## What Safe Pocket does NOT do
- **It has no internet access.** The app is built without Android's internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
- No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no data sales.
- It is excluded from cloud backups, so your documents are not copied to any server.
- Screenshots of the app are blocked to protect your documents.

## Sharing
Safe Pocket only shares a document when you tap **Share** and pick where it goes (for example, to your caseworker). That is always your choice.

## Deleting your data
Use **Settings › Erase everything**, or uninstall the app. Either one permanently deletes everything Safe Pocket stored. Because nothing is kept anywhere else, it cannot be recovered.

## Children
Safe Pocket is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.
