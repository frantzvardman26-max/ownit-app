# Bombs & Bullion (formerly Crown Rush): Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 14, 2026*

Bombs & Bullion is a free game made by Frantz Vardman.

## What the game stores
Only game progress on your phone: best score, gold, stars, missions, unlocked crowns, daily-gift streak, and a count of how often you tapped each "From the maker" card.

## What the game does NOT do
- **It has no internet access.** The game is built without Android's internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
- No account, no ad network, no analytics, no tracking, no data sales, and no in-app purchases.

## "From the maker" cards
The game shows cards for other apps and products by the same maker. Tapping one opens a web page in your browser; nothing is sent by the game itself. Shop links include "ref=crownrush" so the shop can count visits from the game. No personal information is included.

## Deleting your data
Uninstall the game, or clear its storage in your phone's settings.

## Children
Bombs & Bullion collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
