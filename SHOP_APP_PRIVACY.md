# Frantz Vardman Shop app: privacy policy

The Frantz Vardman Shop app shows the shop at shop.frantzmichaelsthilairevardman.com/shop/: products, kits, games and free apps.

- **No tracking in the app:** the app has no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers. It asks only for internet access.
- **Buying:** checkout opens Stripe's own secure page in your browser. The app never sees your card.
- **Your account (optional):** if you sign in, your purchases follow your account so you can download them again. Your email is never sold or shared.
- **Questions:** frantzvardman@mail2one.com
