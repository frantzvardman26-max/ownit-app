# Vindie VPN (Beta): privacy policy

*Last updated: November 20, 2026*

Vindie VPN connects your phone to a private server run by Frantz Vardman. It is in **beta**: invite only, not for sale yet.

## What the server keeps
Your email, a scrambled (hashed) password, each device's name, public key and VPN address, your membership dates, and a record of account actions (joining, agreeing to the rules of use, payments once checkout opens).

## What it does NOT keep
- **No browsing record.** The server's own name lookup runs with query logging off, and the firewall doesn't log what you visit. The account server keeps no request logs.
- **Your private key never leaves your phone.** Each device makes its own key; only the public half is sent.
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no selling data.

## Ad and tracker blocking
The server blocks lookups for known ad, tracker and malware domains (the open StevenBlack hosts list), refreshed weekly by the server itself. Nothing about your browsing is sent anywhere to do this.

## What stays on your phone
Your sign-in, server address and certificate fingerprint, and your tunnel key, sealed with Android's keystore. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

## Deleting your data
Remove a device in the app (Account → Your devices), or ask through the Help form to delete your account.
