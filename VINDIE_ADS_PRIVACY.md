# Vindie Ads (Beta): privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Vindie Ads is an app by Frantz Vardman for booking ads that show on Frantz Vardman's sites and apps. It is in **beta**.

- **What the app is:** a window onto the Vindie Ads website (fv-vindie.pages.dev). It needs internet to book ads and show your ad's numbers.
- **What you give us:** only what you type when you book an ad (your business name, ad text, picture, link and a contact email so we can reach you about your ad). It's used only to run your ad.
- **Payments** are handled by Stripe in your browser. The app never sees your card.
- **No tracking, no selling.** The app adds no analytics or ad trackers. Your information is never sold.
- **Ad views** are counted as simple totals per ad, with no personal details about the people who see it.
