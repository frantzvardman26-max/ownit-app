# Giants & Guns (Beta): privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Giants & Guns is a free game by Frantz Vardman. It is in **beta**: free to play and test, and not for sale yet.

- **Internet is used for one thing only: update checks.** When the app opens it asks the shop which version is newest, so it can offer "Update now". Only the app's name is sent, never anything about you. The game itself is bundled inside the app and plays offline.
- **Nothing is collected.** No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **What stays on your phone:** your gold, upgrades, missions and best room, saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- **Children:** the game collects no personal information from anyone.

Questions: use the Help form at https://shop.frantzmichaelsthilairevardman.com/support/

## Reminders

The game can remind you on this phone when your Ember is full, and 4 hours before it would be lost. The reminder is set and shown by the phone itself: no server, no push service, and nothing about you is sent anywhere. Android asks your permission first, and you can turn reminders off any time in the phone's notification settings for this game. Kids mode sets no reminders.
