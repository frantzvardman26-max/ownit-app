# Giants & Guns (Beta): privacy policy

*Last updated: November 20, 2026*

Giants & Guns is a free game by Frantz Vardman. It is in **beta**: free to play and test, and not for sale.

## What stays on your phone
Your game progress: gold, upgrades, guns, missions, Siege lands and best room, plus settings like sound, music, vibration and button layout. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it.

## What is sent, and only when you choose it
- **Update checks.** When the game opens it asks the shop which version is newest, so it can offer "Update now". Only the app's name is sent, never anything about you.
- **Cloud save (optional).** If you sign in with your Frantz Vardman Shop account, your progress is saved to that account so a new phone or the web version can load it. Not signed in: nothing is saved off your phone.
- **Leaderboard (optional).** If you put a score on the leaderboard, the nickname you type and your score are public. Nicknames are checked for rude words. Nothing else about you is shown.

## What the game does NOT do
No ad network, no analytics, no tracking, no selling data, and no in-app purchases.

## Deleting your data
Uninstall the game or clear its storage. To delete a cloud save or your account, use **Delete my account** at https://shop.frantzmichaelsthilairevardman.com/account/ or ask through the Help form. To remove a leaderboard entry, ask through the Help form.

## Children
The game doesn't ask for personal information. Signing in and leaderboard names are optional.
